# Paid Time Off Policy

## Accrual

Full-time associates accrue 1 hour of PTO for every 26 hours worked, up to 120 hours per year. Part-time associates accrue 1 hour for every 40 hours worked.

## Carryover

Up to 40 hours of unused PTO carries over into the next calendar year.

## Requests

PTO requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance for planned time off.
